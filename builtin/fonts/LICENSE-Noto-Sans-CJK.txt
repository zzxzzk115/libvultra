noto_sans_cjk.otf
=================

This is a SUBSET of Noto Sans CJK SC (Regular), reduced to the GB2312, JIS X 0208
and KS X 1001 character sets plus kana, Latin, common punctuation and the
characters used by this application's localization catalogs. It provides Chinese,
Japanese and Korean glyph coverage for the editor UI.
